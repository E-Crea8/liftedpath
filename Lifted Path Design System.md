# Lifted Path design system

Colors sampled directly from the supplied logo file, typefaces matched to its lettering, and usage rules that keep the brand calm, warm and credible for donors, partners and the communities it serves.

Lifted Path Empowerment Foundation logo

## Color palette

Values are the median of each color region in the 4000 px source, so they reflect the artwork exactly. The logo uses three families: deep teal, gold and a pale sand accent.

## Contrast and accessibility

Teal carries all body copy. The logo gold is beautiful but too light for small text on white, so a darker text gold (#896A38) is added for links and labels.

| Pairing | Ratio | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teal on Paper White | 11.9 : 1 | Passes AAA. All text. |
| Paper White on Teal | 11.9 : 1 | Passes AAA. Buttons, footers, dark sections. |
| Sand on Teal | 6.4 : 1 | Passes AA. Accent text on dark. |
| Text Gold #896A38 on Paper White | 4.9 : 1 | Passes AA. Links, labels, small accents. |
| Path Gold on Teal | 4.0 : 1 | Large text (24 px+) and graphics only. |
| Path Gold or Bronze on Paper White | 3.0 to 3.7 : 1 | Decorative and large display only, never body text. |

## Typography

The wordmark is a soft, high-contrast display serif with rounded, ink-like terminals and a hand-drawn swash on the L. It looks custom drawn, so no font matches it exactly. The tagline is a wide-tracked, flared humanist sans with slightly irregular edges. The families below are the closest open-source matches, all free on Google Fonts.

Display and headings**Fraunces**Weight 700 to 800, Soft axis at 100, optical size auto. Closest match to the wordmark's soft, organic serif.

Taglines and small labels**Marcellus**Regular, letter spacing 0.14em, sentence case or title case. Matches the flared tagline.

Body and interface**Source Sans 3**Regular 400, semibold 600. A neutral humanist sans that reads well on low-end screens and stays quiet beside the display serif.

Keep lines under 70 characters. Headings stay in sentence case. Use teal for text and reserve gold for one accent per section.

## Components

Pill-shaped buttons echo the round sun in the mark. Primary is teal, secondary is text gold with white type, and the ghost button is for low-priority actions. Use a 3 px sand focus ring on every interactive element.

## Logo usage

| Rule | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Clear space | Keep the height of the letter L free on every side. |
| Minimum size | Full lockup 140 px wide on screen, 35 mm in print. Below that, use the circular mark alone. |
| Backgrounds | Full color on Paper White or Gold tints. On teal, use an all-white or all-sand version. |
| Do not | Recolor the teal, stretch the mark, add shadows, or place the full color logo on busy photographs. |

## Design principles

**Rooted and warm.** Deep teal grounds every layout, and gold appears as sunlight, never as a flood.

**Movement forward.** Use gentle curves, rounded shapes and generous white space to echo the winding path in the mark.

**Dignity in imagery.** Photography should show people in action and with agency, in natural light and warm tones.
